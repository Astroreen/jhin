< 1108 < 1123, and jhinFetch(action.path) has exactly 1

text, setFieldError renders it inline, UiState.errors records it, failed
becomes true, and dispatchAction returns at app.js:1108 — zero
round-trips on validation failure (section 3 dispatch order).
