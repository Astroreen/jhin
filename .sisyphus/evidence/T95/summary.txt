# T95 shell chrome summary

shell slots: topbar (52 px token height, gutters + safe-area),
topbarCompact (breadcrumb condensed below 768 px), pageHeader
(title + actions), terminal (grip + 40% narrow cap), stageBackdrop
(the theme.stageArt gradient behind the sidebar under the fade mask)

responsive rule: breakpoint 768 — below it the sidebar overlays as a
drawer (openMobile, backdrop, tap/swipe to close), the right panel is
hidden, the gutters compress to 0.5rem, the terminal caps at 40%

motion: the sidebar open/close rides motion.panel over easeDrawer;
reduced motion collapses the duration to 0

theme.stageArt: the five stage-art hex tokens of the selected theme

goldens: 4 frames (wide light + dark, narrow, notched), byte-stable
on the verify pass
