--- mcp.json schema (documented in McpConfig Javadoc) ---
{"mcpServers": {"<name>": {
  "command": "...", "args": [...], "env": {...},          // transport "stdio"
  "transport": "stdio" | "sse",                            // optional, inferred
  "url": "http://host:port/sse"                            // transport "sse"
}}}
(mcpServers wrapper optional - a bare name->server map is accepted as well)
