T207 traversal matrix — GET through the live kernel (curl --path-as-is,
so dot segments and doubled slashes reach the connector verbatim).

hard rule: none of these may answer 200 with asset bytes (each is
400/404/other non-200); 404 bodies must carry the not_found envelope.
